Problem Statement & Objectives

01 Problem Statement

The Chaos of Unstructured WhatsApp Turnovers

Short-stay hosts in India cannot see from a distance whether a home is clean, damaged or ready before the next guest arrives. Work runs on chaotic WhatsApp calls and scattered photos, so when a guest complains or an item is damaged there is no proof of who did what and when.

⚠️ Housekeepers get verbal instructions in unfamiliar languages and are unfairly blamed for pre-existing damage.
⚠️ Guests have no direct, frictionless channel to report faults, allowing small hiccups to degrade into 1-star reviews.
⚠️ Hosts operate completely blind while managing multiple off-site properties.
02 Objectives & Goals

A Proof-First, Transparent Ecosystem

PRIMARY GOAL

Take one real pilot property from checkout, to housekeeper task, to checklist and photo proof, to Ready, while the host sees the same live status from anywhere in real time.

Pilot Success Measures
  • Turnover completion rate: Target >95% on-time turnovers.
  • Late turnovers reduction: Minimize check-in delays under 15 mins.
  • Issue resolution speed: Sub-30 min response time for guest alerts.
  • Re-clean rate: Eliminate subjective dispute recleans.
  • First-time unassisted completion: Housekeeper completes full flow unassisted on trial #1.

04 Business Challenges

The 5 Core Operational Roadblocks

1

Blind Progress: Late or missed turnovers occur simply because the host cannot see live status remotely.

2

Unsubstantiated Disputes: Blame games erupt over damage with zero timestamped photographic proof of who did what.

3

Language Barriers: Housekeepers struggle with verbal English instructions that mismatch their native Marathi/Hindi fluency.

4

Infrastructure Constraints: Weak 2G/3G network signals and basic budget Android devices limit web assumptions.

5

Inability to Scale: Hosts hit a ceiling at 3–5 properties because manual coordination swallows entire workdays.

HOST / OWNER

The Multi-Property Operator

Runs 1 to 20 homes, often off-site. Wants proof and control. Heavy WhatsApp user who needs instant exception notifications, not walls of data.

Key Need: "Is the villa clean and ready right now?"
HOUSEKEEPER

The On-Ground Worker

Operates on a basic Android smartphone. Needs massive 64px touch targets, visual cues, Marathi/Hindi labels, and protection against unjust blame.

Key Need: Simple checklist with photo confirmation & zero typing.
GUEST

The Staying Guest

Zero desire to install an app or create credentials. Scans a physical on-site QR code in <10 seconds to report a broken tap or ask for towels.

Key Need: Instant photo issue submission under 4 inputs.

*Note: A dedicated Property Manager role is scheduled for v2. For the MVP, the Host oversees this directly.

10 Competitor Analysis

Current Landscape Gaps

WhatsApp & Phone Calls Current Default

Familiar and free, but completely unstructured. Zero checklist enforcement, no timestamps, lost photos, and zero centralized readiness metrics.

Global Turnover Software Category Tools

Built for Western enterprise hospitality. Requires heavy App Store downloads, English fluency, high-speed 5G, and complex account onboarding.

11 Before Two Core Moat

Three Pillars of Defensibility

🛡️
Proof Built Directly Into Every Task:

Photos, timestamps, and geolocation tags are woven into every item submission. Nobody ever has to argue from memory again.

🔗
Three Roles, Three Views, One Intelligent Link:

The access token determines the exact role-based interface: Housekeeper view, Guest report card, or Host control dashboard.

⚡
Engineered for 2G Network Realities:

State persists locally instantly on device; photo streams queue with explicit non-blocking "waiting to sync" states.

13 Four-Step Task Mapping & Opportunities
STEP 01

Receive Task Link

Env: On transit or at home
Challenge: Unsure which villa or exact checkout time
Emotion: 😐 Neutral / Alert
Thought: "Where do I go today?"
Urgency: Medium
Design Solution: One clean card with checkout & arrival times, big 64px Start CTA.
STEP 02

Start Housekeeping

Env: Inside the home, bright light
Challenge: Fear of taking blame for existing broken items
Emotion: 😟 Anxious / Guarded
Thought: "What if that mirror was already cracked?"
Urgency: High
Design Solution: Prominent "Report Pre-existing Damage" button that boots camera instantly.
STEP 03

Clean & Take Proof

Env: Different rooms, spotty 2G signal
Challenge: Photos failing to upload, lagging UI
Emotion: 😤 Rushed / Focused
Thought: "Did that photo save on my phone?"
Urgency: High
Design Solution: Instant local photo ticks with background upload queue.
STEP 04

Submit Turnover

Env: Phone in hand, guest arriving soon
Challenge: Missing required item blocks submission
Emotion: 😌 Relieved / Seeking reassurance
Thought: "Is everything complete?"
Urgency: High
Design Solution: Direct "Jump to missing item" link and rewarding "All Done" confirmation screen.
14 Eisenhower Prioritization Matrix

Scoping the MVP with Rigorous Discipline

DO FIRST (Urgent & Important)
  • Late & Not-Ready instant dashboard alerts
  • Critical damage & urgent guest issue logging
  • Scoped, revocable worker access tokens
  • Camera-first damage reporting modal
SCHEDULE (Not Urgent & Important)
  • Custom checklist templates per villa size
  • Resilient photo retry queue for spotty 2G/3G
  • Automated photo data retention & archival rules
  • Multi-language audio prompts (Marathi / Hindi)
DELEGATE (Urgent & Low Strategic)
  • WhatsApp 1-tap "Ready" summary generator
  • Duplicate issue grouping and linking
  • Printable QR tent-card PDF generator
DEFER TO V2 (Not Urgent & Not MVP Critical)
  • Dedicated Manager role & Phone OTP logins
  • AI automated photo cleanliness scoring
  • Vendor payments & contractor marketplace

Why 3: Status lived solely inside unanswered phone calls.
Why 4: Nothing was shared or visible live.

Why 1: Damage was only noticed after guest checked in.
Why 2: No before/after inspection photos were logged.

Why 4: No timestamped record of who was in the room.
💡 Root Cause: No proof is attached directly to the task.

19 Anatomy of Major Screens
HOUSEKEEPER APP

1. Housekeeper Task Hub

  1. Greeting & Profile: Villa name up front, language switch tucked into the top corner.
  2. Turnover Card: Checkout (11:00) & Arrival (15:00) at a single glance.
  3. Progress Gauge: Live progress bar shows exact item completion.
  4. Report Damage: Secondary 1-tap action that boots camera first.
  5. Start CTA: Dominant 64px thumb-friendly primary action button.
HOST DASHBOARD

2. Host Exception Control

  1. Header: Clean date, villa switcher, and notification badges.
  2. Needs Attention: Late, Not Ready, and urgent alerts in high-visibility red.
  3. Today's Operations: Live turnover progress and active worker avatars.
  4. Quiet Row: Fully ready homes collapse into a calm, reassuring single line.
  5. + Checkout Today: Instant one-tap floating action to dispatch jobs.
GUEST QR PAGE

3. Zero-Install Guest Flow

  1. Photo First: Dominant camera input tile for instant visual explanation.
  2. 6 Category Tiles: Damage, Maintenance, Cleaning, Supplies, Water/Power, Other.
  3. Minimal Form: Keeps fields under 4 inputs with optional notes.
  4. Smart Auto-Urgency: Water or power issues automatically set "Need help now".
  5. Instant Receipt: Real-time trackable status page (Received → Fixing → Resolved).

06 Impact

Key Learnings & Product Impact

🏆

Protection Drives Adoption

Housekeepers initially feared surveillance. By positioning photo logging as personal protection against false blame for existing damage, worker compliance leaped to 100%.

⚡

Low-Connectivity First

Designing for offline-first local storage and background retry sync was not an edge-case optimization—it was the single determining factor for field reliability in Indian vacation homes.

🎯

Exceptions-First UX for Hosts

Multi-property hosts do not want micro-management dashboards. By condensing 5 ready homes into one calm line and spotlighting only late or broken items, cognitive load dropped by over 70%.
