I design digital products with clarity, character, and real-world usability in mind.

I am a Product Designer and UI UX Designer based in Pune. I design websites, mobile apps, SaaS dashboards, admin panels, and AI-assisted products for businesses that need clarity, usability, and strong execution.

I enjoy working at the point where user needs, business goals, and technical realities meet. My focus is not only on making interfaces look clean, but also on making them practical, scalable, and easy for users and development teams to work with.

How I think about design

Good design is not just a polished screen. To me, it is a way to reduce confusion, structure information, and help people take the right action with confidence.

A lot of my work involves products that are complex by nature - real estate platforms, healthcare experiences, B2B tools, admin dashboards, e-commerce websites, AI products, and internal business systems. In these projects, clarity matters more than decoration.

Design also does not happen in perfect conditions. There are deadlines, business constraints, developer limitations, unclear requirements, and changing feedback. I see these as part of the work. My goal is to understand the problem quickly, make smart design decisions, and move the product forward with practical execution.
