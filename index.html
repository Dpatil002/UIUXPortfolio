What I do

01

Design Leadership

Lead and mentor junior UI/UX designers on design thinking, problem-solving, and craft — building team capability while owning delivery quality end to end.

02

End-to-End Product Design

Own the full product design lifecycle — research, user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI — for web and mobile products across fintech, retail, wellness, and community platforms.

03

AI Augmented Development

Bridge design and engineering using AI-assisted build tools (Claude Code, Antigravity, Figma Make, v0) to ship production-ready interfaces faster, with design fidelity intact.

04

Design Systems & QC

Build and maintain scalable design systems and lead design-to-development QC handover, ensuring pixel-accurate, consistent implementation before delivery.

05

Stakeholder Management

Manage client and stakeholder communication directly — translating business requirements into design decisions and keeping delivery aligned with expectations and deadlines.

06

Project & Deadline Ownership

Own full project timelines end to end, from scoping through launch, balancing team workload, quality, and delivery speed across concurrent projects.
