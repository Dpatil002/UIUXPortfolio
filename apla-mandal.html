Apla Mandal

One app for a 31-year-old Ganeshotsav

Neighbours pay vargani, check tonight's aarti and relive the photos. Organisers keep every contribution on record. Four tabs, in English and Marathi.

Role
Product designer and builder
Platform
Mobile web
Client
Indrayani Vihar Mitra Mandal, Pune
Status
Live for the 2026 festival

Overview

A festival run by neighbours

Indrayani Vihar Mitra Mandal in Lohegaon, Pune was founded in 1995. Its Ganeshotsav runs for twelve days, from 14 to 25 September in 2026, with daily aartis, cultural events and a list of mankari families.

I designed and built the app for my own mandal: a home screen, a bilingual schedule, a four-step vargani flow and a PIN-protected organiser side. I then refreshed the interface into a lighter, clearer look.

Challenge

Everyone needs something different at night

Children, parents and elders all open the same app. Some need the aarti time in large type. Some need to pay and know it was noted. Organisers need a name, an amount and a receiver for every entry.

The brief I set myself: let any neighbour pay vargani and find the aarti time in under a minute, in the language they read best.

4 tabsHome, Schedule, Pay Vargani, Mandal
4 stepsDonor, amount, QR, confirm
2 languagesEnglish and Marathi on every label
10 screensIn 3 flows, redesigned in light

Approach

Familiar patterns, plain words

Audit

What I fixed after testing the live app myself

I opened every tab at phone width, measured tap targets and checked each label against the schedule. Three findings changed the design.

Touch targets

Call and chat buttons were 32px. They are now 44px.

Day count

The header said 11 days beside 12 day chips. It now says 12.

Colour contrast

The maroon is deepened so text on cream clears 8 to 1.

Outcome

Live, audited, ready to test

The app served the 2026 festival. The light redesign is drawn and checked for contrast, and its test plan has pass marks set in advance. Usability tests with residents and volunteers are the next step, so I make no result claims yet.
