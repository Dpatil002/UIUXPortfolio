01

Problem Statement

Most beginners who start a fitness journey quit within weeks. Not because the plan was wrong, but because one missed day becomes a missed week.

They don't know what exercises to perform, what to eat that matches home-cooked Indian meals, or who to ask without feeling intimidated. Generic fitness apps serve static plans with overwhelming configuration, zero empathy, and no one standing in their corner on bad days.

Business Challenges

Balancing commercial viability with product adoption in India

Retention

Fitness apps lose over 70% of new users in the first month. In fitness, daily consistency is the whole game.

Trust in AI

Beginners must genuinely believe an AI plan is safe, physiologically sound, and tailored specifically to them.

Coach Care at Scale

Delivering warm, personal attention and accountability for thousands of lifters without hiring 1-on-1 human trainers.

Local Relevance

Western meal templates (avocado toast, plain chicken breast) fail in Indian households. Diets must be Indian-first.

Price Sensitivity

Monetisation must feel earned and fair in India's price-conscious market. Deliver undeniable daily value before paywalls.

Content Cost

Filming hundreds of video demos and recipes is expensive. Version 1 leverages lightweight illustrated motion demos.

A safe companion who answers form and nutrition doubts in relatable, non-intimidating plain words.

Product User Challenges

Friction points that destroy early fitness momentum

Friction 01

Conflicting Advice

Reels, gym bros, and online forums give contradictory advice on sets, reps, and macros, leaving beginners paralysed.

Friction 02

Gym Floor Anxiety

Intimidation from crowded barbell racks, unfamiliar cable setups, and fear of looking foolish during execution.

Friction 03

Irregular Energy

Commutes, exams, and overtime work make standard 60-minute training routines unrealistic multiple days a week.

Friction 04

Post-Skip Guilt

Traditional apps trigger red error states and notifications that induce shame, speeding up the decision to quit entirely.

Unique Features (The Raya Moat)

Four foundational pillars designed specifically for Indian lifters

Feature 01

The 5-Minute Skip Rescue

Tapping "Can't do it today?" converts an impending skip into a 5-minute micro-session tailored to low energy, lack of time, or muscle soreness. The user's streak survives, preventing the snowball drop-off effect.

Feature 02

Plans That Trace Back to You

Every exercise and meal generated by Raya explicitly shows its reasoning tied to the user's onboarding answers (available equipment, injury history, and schedule). Users trust the plan because they see why it was chosen.

Feature 03

Indian-Only Diet by Design

Strict adherence to Indian dietary categories (Vegetarian, Non-Veg, Egg, Jain). Raya calculates macros around rotis, dal, paneer, and rice without forcing alien Western grocery lists onto family kitchens.

Feature 04

Opt-in Monthly Progression

Raya reviews verified monthly adherence before proposing an upgraded training split. It never silently swaps routines without explicit permission, ensuring users feel in complete control of their journey.

5-Why Analysis & Root Cause Analysis

Uncovering the psychological truths behind beginner dropout

Problem 1: Beginners quit within a month

1 They skip one training day, which snowballs into another.
2 A full 60-minute workout feels utterly overwhelming on tired days.
3 Fitness apps provide an all-or-nothing binary choice.
4 Plans are static and completely ignore fluctuating human energy.
5 Apps measure workout volume rather than habit survival.
Root Cause Identified: Lack of a Minimum-Viable Workout option on low-energy days.

Problem 2: Users don't trust AI fitness plans

1 The generated plan feels like a generic template copy-paste.
2 Exercises don't match the specific machines in their gym.
3 Suggested meals don't match what their family cooks at home.
4 No visible link connects their onboarding answers to the output.
5 The product never proves that it actually listened to the user.
Root Cause Identified: The generated output fails to visibly prove why it was tailored.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Mapping

1. No Clear Plan → Overwhelming Online Advice → Root: No single trusted coach
2. Missed Days Snowball → All-or-Nothing Sessions → Root: No 5-minute rescue option
3. Diet Doesn't Fit → Western Meal Templates → Root: Not built India-first
4. Fear of Asking → Gym Floor Intimidation → Root: No non-judgemental mentor
5. Plans Go Stale → No Natural Progression → Root: App ignores consistency

Key Design Learnings

Reflections from designing an empathetic fitness ecosystem

Learning 01

Consistency is Emotional, Not Logistical

Beginners do not drop out because they lack complex barbell periodisation. They quit because shame and all-or-nothing binary designs make recovery feel hopeless after a missed day. Giving them a 5-minute win changes everything.

Learning 02

Cultural Grounding Builds Deep Trust

AI applications cannot feel generic. By designing specifically around Indian staples (paneer, dal, roti) and writing microcopy in the warm, respectful cadence of an Indian elder brother ("bhai"), the app feels deeply human and local.
